Know exactly what you owe the milkman

Milk Ledger is a simple Android app for households that get milk delivered every day. Tap the days it came, and the month's bill adds itself up.

  • Mark each dayTap once for received, twice for not received. Separate tabs for 1 litre and ½ litre.
  • Bill at a glanceSet your own rate for 1 litre and ½ litre, and the bill is totalled for the calendar month.
  • Pay by UPIOpens Google Pay, PhonePe or any UPI app with the amount filled in. You confirm with your own PIN.
  • Synced and privateSign in with Google. Your records follow you to a new phone, and no other household can see them.

Milk Ledger never handles your money or bank details. Payments go directly from your UPI app to the milkman.
