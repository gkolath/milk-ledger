What the app does

Milk Ledger is a free tool for recording daily milk deliveries, working out the monthly bill at the rates shown in the app (₹70 a day for 1 litre, ₹35 a day for ½ litre), and opening your UPI app to pay the milkman.

Your records are your responsibility

The bill is calculated only from the days you mark. Check your entries before you pay. If your records and the milkman's differ, settle it directly with the milkman. Milk Ledger is not a party to your arrangement with them.

Payments

Payments are made in your own UPI app, between you and the milkman. Milk Ledger does not process, hold or refund money, and cannot reverse a payment. For a problem with a payment, contact your UPI app or bank. "Mark as paid" is a note for your own records and is not proof of payment.
