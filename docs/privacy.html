Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026

This policy explains how the Milk Ledger Android app ("Milk Ledger", "we", "us") handles your information. Milk Ledger helps a household record daily milk deliveries, see the monthly bill and pay the milk seller by UPI.

The short version

Milk Ledger has no accounts and no servers. Everything you enter stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and we never receive it.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Seller details: the milk seller's name, their UPI ID and the rates you enter in Settings.
  • Delivery records: the dates you mark, whether milk was received or not, whether it was 1 litre or ½ litre, and the rate on that day.
  • Payment records: when you tap "Mark as paid", the month, the amount and whether you paid by UPI or cash.

This information is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not included in Android's cloud backup. Insights, the forecast, your usual pattern, alerts and the quick-add box all work out their results on your phone too.

What the app does not collect

No sign-in, name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos. No ads, no analytics and no tracking. Unless you turn on on-phone AI (below), the app makes no internet connections of its own; it only opens a link in your browser when you tap one, such as this policy.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the reminder in Settings, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications and schedules "Did milk come today?" reminders on your phone. Answering with Received or Not received marks the day on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

On-phone AI (optional, off by default)

On phones that have Google's Gemini Nano, you can turn on on-phone AI in Settings → On-phone AI. It lets you type notes in your own words, ask questions about your records and get a warmer bill message. The AI model runs on your phone: what you type, your questions and your records are processed on the device and are not sent to us or to Google. Android may download the model from Google the first time you set it up.

While on-phone AI is on, the app uses Google's ML Kit, which sends Google basic usage statistics: your phone's model and Android version, the app's name and version, an identifier used for diagnostics, the languages configured, and speed and error figures. Google says it sends this over an encrypted connection and does not share it with third parties; see ML Kit's data disclosure and the Google privacy policy. Turn on-phone AI off in Settings at any time to stop this.

UPI payments and sharing

When you tap "Pay with UPI", Milk Ledger opens the UPI app you choose, such as Google Pay or PhonePe, with the seller's UPI ID, the amount and a short note filled in. You complete the payment in that app. Milk Ledger never sees or stores your bank account, card, UPI PIN or transaction details; your UPI app and bank handle those under their own privacy policies.

When you tap "Share bill", the app opens your phone's share menu with the bill text. It goes only to the app and person you choose.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your records stay on your phone until you remove them. To delete everything, go to Settings → Clear all data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings also deletes it. Because nothing is stored with us, we cannot recover lost records or move them to a new phone.

Children

Milk Ledger is meant for adults managing a household. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, show a notice in the app.
